# Rokt documentation

> Official documentation for Rokt Ecommerce partners and Rokt Ads advertisers.

[Rokt Ecommerce](https://docs.rokt.com/products/ecommerce/) is the partner-facing product suite for ecommerce businesses integrating Rokt into transaction experiences they own. [Rokt Ads](https://docs.rokt.com/products/ads/) is the advertiser-facing acquisition channel across the Rokt Network.

## Rokt Ecommerce partner integrations

- [Rokt Ecommerce SDK+ integration guides](https://main--docs-rokt.netlify.app/integration-guides/ecommerce/sdk/llms.txt): Complete platform-specific implementation guides for ecommerce partners.

## Rokt Ads advertiser integrations

- [Rokt Ads integration guides](https://docs.rokt.com/integration-guides/ads/): Guidance for advertisers connecting conversion data and measuring Rokt Ads campaigns.
